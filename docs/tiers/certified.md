## Modules unlocked (lesson + milestone)

### Module 1 — Wallet foundations

- **Coach beats:** Keys + address; receive before you send; watch the balance, not the jargon.
- **What they practice:** Create practice wallet → confirm receive → send a small practice amount.
- **Milestone spec:** Simulated wallet dashboard; must complete create, receive (≥0.25 practice ETH), and send (≤ balance) before certify.
- **Success criteria:** Address shown; balance updated after receive; send reduces balance; name entered → cert issued.
- **Cert:** “Module 1 — Wallet Basics · Create · Receive · Send (simulated)”

### Module 2 — Exchange & spot basics

- **Coach beats:** Parking garage vs owning the car. Market = now; limit = your price. Wrong network = lost luggage.
- **What they practice:** Place a simulated spot buy; pick the matching deposit network.
- **Milestone specs:**
  1. Spot order — choose market or limit, enter size, confirm fill in simulation.
  2. Network check — match asset to the correct deposit network (reject the mismatch).
- **Success criteria:** Order confirmed in sim; correct network selected on first or retry; then certify.
- **Cert:** “Module 2 — Exchange & Spot · Order + network check (simulated)”

### Module 3 — Self-custody

- **Coach beats:** Not your keys, not your coins. Seed phrase never screenshots or cloud. Verify receive address before you fund for real.
- **What they practice:** Generate simulated seed reveal (practice words); confirm offline-storage rules; show a practice receive address.
- **Milestone specs:**
  1. Seed protocol — acknowledge “write offline / never photo / never paste in chat.”
  2. Address ownership — display practice receive address and confirm it matches the sim wallet.
- **Success criteria:** Checklist complete; address confirmed; certify.
- **Cert:** “Module 3 — Self-Custody · Seed hygiene + address (simulated)”

### Module 4 — Risk & hygiene

- **Coach beats:** Fake support is the curb. Approvals can drain. Always send a tiny test first.
- **What they practice:** Spot phishing vs legit cues; run a simulated 0.001 test transfer before a larger send.
- **Milestone specs:**
  1. Scam spot — pick the safe action when shown a fake-support / drainer scenario.
  2. Test transfer — send tiny amount first in sim, then unlock the larger send.
- **Success criteria:** Safe choice selected; test transfer completed before full send; certify.
- **Cert:** “Module 4 — Risk & Hygiene · Scam spot + test transfer (simulated)”
